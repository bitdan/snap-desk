Snap Desk (also written SnapDesk) is a free and open-source cross-platform desktop utility for screenshots, scrolling capture, annotation, image pinning, MP4/GIF recording, OCR, translation, and clipboard history.

Features

Everything between taking a screenshot and putting it to use happens in the same app.

Capture & annotate

Capture regions, screens, or windows. Add arrows, text, numbered labels, blur, and mosaic; export PNG, JPEG, or WebP.

Scrolling capture

Stitch manually scrolled vertical or horizontal content, preview captures, and export long PNG images.

Pin to desktop

Keep screenshots, images, or clipboard content on top of your desktop; adjust opacity, zoom, or annotations.

Screen recording

Record a screen or region to MP4 / GIF with pause/resume, live annotations, and input visualization. MP4 supports system and microphone audio.

OCR & translation

Choose offline OCR engines such as PaddleOCR or Windows OCR, or configure optional online recognition and translation APIs.

Clipboard history

Find text, images, and file paths with categories, search, favorites, preview, and export options.
